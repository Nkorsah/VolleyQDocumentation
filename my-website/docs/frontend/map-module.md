# Map Module

Handles map rendering and venue markers.

---

## initMap()

Initializes the Google Map instance.

---

## getVenueData()

Fetches volleyball venue data.

---

## loadVenueMarkers(venues)

Adds markers to the map.

---

## createMarker(lat, lng, icon, info)

Creates a single marker.

---

## showInfoWindow(marker, content)

Displays venue info.

---

## updateMarkers(newVenues)

Clears and redraws markers.
